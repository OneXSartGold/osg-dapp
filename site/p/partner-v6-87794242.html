Rules

  • Ceilings, not promises. Every % is a maximum. If the pot runs short, all rates shrink in the same proportion; when demand falls they return to the full set rate, never above it.
  • Unused share is shared. If one part does not use its share on a day, it goes wherever another part is short (levels, rank or spot). Budget nobody uses is not minted.
  • Adjustable without a new contract. The owner can change any level %, the directs and rank needed, the number of active levels (up to 50), rank terms, Spot terms and the pot split in one signed change, within hard limits written into the contract.
  • 48-hour notice. Every change is announced to the community at least 48 hours before it is applied.
  • Spot has no end date. It can be switched on or off by the owner.
  • One system. v5 and the current Spot contract are closed; every member's team, balances owed, rank and Spot history move into v6 unchanged.
