About OSG.

OneX Smart Gold began with a simple idea: a community token should be as easy to check as it is to use.

OSG is built and run by the OSG Team together with its community. Instead of asking people to trust a website, every rule — how much OSG is released each day, how it is shared, how long LP is locked — lives in a verified smart contract on Polygon. The DApp, OSGScan and this site all read the same public data.

We ship in small, careful steps: each change is tested, reviewed and announced before it goes live, and rate changes are announced at least 48 hours in advance.
