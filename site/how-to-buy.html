How to get OSG

Six short steps with a Polygon wallet. Take your time and check every address.

  1. 1

    Install a wallet

    Install a wallet that supports Polygon (for example MetaMask or Bitget Wallet). Write the recovery phrase on paper; never share it with anyone.

  2. 2

    Switch to Polygon

    Make sure the wallet is on the Polygon (PoS) network.

  3. 3

    Get a little POL

    Get a little POL for network fees and for the swap.

  4. 4

    Open the DApp

    Open https://app.onexsmartgold.com inside the wallet's browser.

  5. 5

    Swap POL for OSG

    Connect, open Swap, and swap POL for OSG. Check that the token contract is:

    0xba05176748347944CC26900c821AbFeBeBC57415

  6. 6

    Add OSG to your wallet

    Tap "Add OSG to wallet" so OSG shows in the wallet. You can also copy the token address and add it by hand (symbol OSG, 18 decimals).

Good to know

  • Keep about 2 POL in the wallet for network fees (gas).
  • Prices move both ways. The price of OSG can go up or down.
  • Only use official links. See Official links & safety.
  • Do your own research (DYOR) before you take part.
