FAQ

Questions people ask.

Still unsure? The AI assistant inside the DApp can explain any part in plain words.

Which network is OSG on?

Polygon PoS (chain id 137). You need a little POL in your wallet to pay network fees.

How do I add OSG to my wallet?

Use "Add OSG to wallet" at the top of this page, or add it by hand: address 0xba05176748347944CC26900c821AbFeBeBC57415, symbol OSG, 18 decimals.

Where do LP mining rewards come from?

From the Reward Pool's fixed daily release. The 68% Mining share is divided between the LP programmes by on-chain shares. The figures shown are ceilings and fall when more LP joins.

Can I take my LP out before the lock ends?

No. LP stays locked until the end of the period you chose (180, 365 or 540 days). Rewards can be claimed along the way.

Can the rates change?

Yes. Reward ceilings can change and the rate actually paid floats with participation. The team announces every rate change at least 48 hours before it applies; on the newest LP contract that delay is enforced on-chain.

Is it safe?

All crypto carries risk. The contracts are verified and public, but smart contracts can have bugs and token prices move. Only use what you can afford to lose, and never share your seed phrase.
