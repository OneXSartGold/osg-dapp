Contract facts.

What the OSG token contract on Polygon can and cannot do.

The token

  • Open-source and verified on Polygonscan.
  • Not upgradable.
  • No blacklist.
  • 0% buy/sell tax.
  • Holders' balances cannot be changed by the team.

Disclosed powers

  • The Reward Pool can mint the scheduled daily release. Minting is hard-capped: at most 23,000,000 OSG in total, 500 OSG per hour and 9,000 OSG per day.
  • A new minter can only be added after a 72-hour wait.
  • An emergency pause exists for incidents. Only governance can unpause.
